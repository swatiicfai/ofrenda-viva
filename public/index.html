Why Open Innovation Matters for Ofrenda Viva

Sacred Privacy of Grief

Photos, letters, and voice memories of departed family members must never be indexed for ad targeting or used to train corporate cloud models. Open weights mean data never leaves your device.

Digital Immortality Without Paywalls

A digital memorial should not disappear when a SaaS startup pivots or a credit card expires. Running open models locally guarantees that the ofrenda lives on your personal hardware forever.

Gemma & Open Harness Power

Google's Gemma open weights deliver frontier-grade poetic nuance and cultural resonance right on commodity consumer laptops with zero API costs.
